    Know what actually helps

    • Use the phone maker's built-in Game Mode when available.
    • Lower shadows, effects, and render resolution before sacrificing a stable frame rate.
    • Heat causes throttling. Take breaks and avoid playing while charging if the phone gets hot.
    • Keep enough free storage for updates and temporary files.
    • Use a stable Wi-Fi connection. Network lag is different from low FPS.

    Avoid “RAM cleaner”, “FPS unlocker”, and unknown booster apps that promise guaranteed FPS. This app does not change CPU/GPU clocks, bypass game limits, or modify protected Android settings.
